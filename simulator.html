    Simulation of the method in Reflection-Based Relative Localization for Cooperative UAV Teams Using Active Markers. The image is synthesised: an equidistant fisheye stands in for the calibrated OCam model, and the reflection is a glitter pattern from Gaussian facet slopes with Fresnel reflectance. Everything after the image follows Sec. II: blob centroids, ellipse extremes, the two elliptical cones, uniform sampling of the reflection cone, the inlier test against the direct cone, and a constant-velocity particle filter with a Cauchy kernel and ESS-triggered resampling. Method parameters default to Tab. I; scene heights and speeds are illustrative.
